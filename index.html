我是杭州电子科技大学自动化学院控制工程硕士生，现于 RAS Lab 开展机器人鲁棒感知研究。I am an M.Eng. student in Control Engineering at Hangzhou Dianzi University, working on robust robot perception at the RAS Lab.

我关注复杂、非结构化真实环境中的可靠感知，使用深度学习或几何约束方法，缓解雨、雾、雪与沙尘条件下 LiDAR 传感器的数据退化。目前计划在机器人感知与自主系统方向继续攻读博士学位。My work focuses on reliable perception in complex, unstructured environments. I use deep learning or geometric-constraint-based methods to mitigate LiDAR degradation in rain, fog, snow, and dust. I plan to pursue a PhD in robot perception and autonomous systems.

目标：Goal: 提升机器人在复杂真实环境中的感知可靠性。Improve the reliability of robot perception in complex real-world environments.

研究兴趣：Research Interest: 关于机器人的一切Everything about robots

研究问题：Research Question: 如何使用深度学习或几何约束方法，缓解恶劣天气下的传感器数据退化？How can deep learning or geometric-constraint-based methods mitigate sensor degradation in adverse weather?

研究对象：Research Objects: LiDAR、三维点云、点云去噪、移动机器人LiDAR, 3D Point Clouds, Point Cloud Denoising, Mobile Robots
