<div style="font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  目前于杭州电子科技大学自动化学院攻读硕士学位，在 Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS) Lab 开展科研工作。<br>
  研究聚焦于<b>非结构化与极端气象环境下的机器人鲁棒感知问题</b>，围绕激光雷达与视觉传感器在雨、雾、雪、沙尘等复杂条件下的<b>感知退化建模、噪声抑制与信息恢复</b>展开研究。致力于结合<b>深度学习方法与几何约束建模</b>，提升移动机器人在真实环境中的全天候稳定运行能力。

研究方向
======
* **全天候环境下的鲁棒感知 (Robust Perception in Adverse Weather)**: 
  * **多模态传感器去噪与复原**: 针对激光雷达 (LiDAR) 与视觉 (Vision) 在雨、雾、雪、沙尘环境下的退化复原 (Restoration) 与抗扰算法 (Noise Mitigation) 研究。
* **多模态传感器融合 (LiDAR–Vision Fusion)**
* **几何约束下的深度学习方法**
* **移动机器人感知系统工程实现**

<div style="font-size: 1.1em; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 20px;">
  I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Control Engineering at Hangzhou Dianzi University, conducting research in the <b>Robotics and Autonomous Systems (RAS) Lab</b>.<br>
  My research focuses on <b>robust perception for mobile robots in unstructured and adverse environments</b>, investigating degradation modeling, noise suppression, and data restoration for LiDAR and vision sensors under challenging conditions such as rain, fog, snow, and dust. I aim to integrate <b>deep learning methods with geometric constraint modeling</b> to improve all-weather perception stability for real-world robotic systems.
</div>

Research Interests
======
* **Robust Perception in Adverse Weather**:
  * **Sensor Noise Suppression & Data Restoration**: Degradation recovery and interference mitigation for LiDAR and vision sensors under rain, fog, snow, and dust.
* **Multi-modal Sensor Fusion (LiDAR–Vision)**
* **Geometry-aware Deep Learning**
* **Perception System Engineering for Mobile Robots**
